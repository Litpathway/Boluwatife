# Daniel Folorunsho

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

One standalone HTML document at `dist/index.html`, with inline CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The source lives in `src/index.html`, `src/styles.css` and `src/app.js`. `scripts/build.ps1` embeds Manrope and the supplied images, then writes the standalone page and its font license. Open the built page directly in a browser; there are no runtime dependencies or external asset requests.

## Users

Potential partners, pilot customers, investors, collaborators, and people following Daniel’s work. They need to understand who he is, what he builds, the experience behind it, and how to contact him.

## Product Purpose

A personal founder website for Daniel Folorunsho, known as Bolu. Its purpose is to make his work understandable and give interested visitors a direct way to start a conversation.

## Positioning

Daniel is the founder of Zerall Ltd in Lagos, Nigeria. He builds software for businesses trading between China and Nigeria, informed by firsthand customer-service and operational-communication work inside a Lagos import/export business.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Zerall is the parent software company.
- Hub is a Flutter-based business operations platform with a separate workspace for each trade business.
- Kova is an offline desktop application for shipping manifests, packing lists, and bills through WhatsApp.
- Contact uses the founder-provided LinkedIn profile. No verified email address is available.
- TikTok and Instagram use the supplied `@bolu_niel` handle and existing profile destinations.
- Do not invent launch status, product URLs, customers, revenue, investment, testimonials, results, seniority, or availability.
- Preserve the professional and education facts in `research.md`. The Miva degree is in progress, with expected completion in May 2029.

## Brand Commitments

- The user selected adapting the supplied Oliver Scott reference for Daniel. The current direction uses bright teal #00BFA6, deep teal #008F7A, near-black #050505, soft white #F7F7F7, white #FFFFFF and muted gray #777777, with Manrope, capsule navigation and actions, a layered portrait hero, a black ticker and five accordion rows. The user-directed palette change preserves the selected composition and supersedes the earlier vermilion accents.
- The original supplied portrait remains an unchanged transparent cutout, layered above a separate CSS bright-teal disc. Preserve Daniel's face and the image's original pixels.
- Daniel's close-up photo, longer name, actual Hub/Kova screenshots and factual founder copy intentionally differ from the reference.
- Use direct first-person language and concrete workflow descriptions.
- Maintain the current light visual system, mobile navigation, accessible controls and reduced-motion support.

## Product Principles

- Make Daniel and the purpose of his products clear immediately.
- Let actual product screens and firsthand experience carry credibility.
- Explain software through the work it supports.

- Prefer accurate, unfinished product evidence to invented proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow WCAG 2.2 AA principles, with keyboard access, visible focus, semantic landmarks, useful image descriptions, appropriate contrast, 44px controls where practical, and usable layouts from 320px upward.
